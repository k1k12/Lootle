THE PLAN

  • Every safe has lots of right answers. Give one.
  • The fewer people who say yours, the further the dial turns.
  • 30 seconds and 3 tries per safe.
  • Grab it to keep your loot, or risk it: one more answer. Rarer swaps you up. Anything else trips the alarm and the safe pays nothing.
  • You get 10 seconds to choose, then 20 seconds for the risk answer.
  • Safes get harder as the night goes on. The last one is the vault: a specialist list most people cannot crack cold.
  • Everything you take goes into your stash. Spend it at the fence on junk to throw, extra seconds, or dynamite.

Rarity starts as an estimate and shifts with every real answer.
